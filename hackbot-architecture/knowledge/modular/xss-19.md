CLASS: authentication bypass / privilege escalation via TLS SESSION-TICKET RESUMPTION not isolated per virtual host (mTLS context confusion — a resumed handshake skips the vhost's client-auth policy).
CORE METHOD (the report's verbatim findings):

Tell on the wire: a second connection to vhost B whose handshake is RESUMED (`ssl_session_reused=1`, `SSL re-use`, abbreviated handshake) even though the client certificate presented would be rejected on a fresh handshake.
