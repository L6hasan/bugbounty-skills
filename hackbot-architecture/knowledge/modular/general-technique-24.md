CLASS: BAC / authorization-revocation bypass — a signed capability URL (`path_hmac`) whose HMAC authorizes a state-changing action but is bound to NEITHER the session/user NOR time (it never expires), so removing the staff member's permission does NOT revoke the URL.

THE REVOCATION-BYPASS, verbatim: "it's possible for a staff member who had \"Apps\" permission then was removed to modify the connection settings for Google SpreadSheets, Trello and Asana by just saving the `path_hmac`." Steps: add staff with only "Apps" permission -> install Flow -> as that staff open `https://[shop].myshopify.com/admin/apps/flow/connectors` -> click Settings next to Google Sheets / Trello / Asana and SAVE the returned `https://flow-connectors.shopifycloud.com/gsheet/connect?shop_domain=...&shop_id=...&path_hmac=...` links -> owner removes the staff -> "You can now use the links you saved to modify connectors settings."
